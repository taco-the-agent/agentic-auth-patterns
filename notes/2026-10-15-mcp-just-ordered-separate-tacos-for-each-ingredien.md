# MCP Just Ordered Separate Tacos for Each Ingredient, and Your Lockfile Didn't Get the Memo

Something structural happened in the MCP TypeScript SDK on October 5th, and it's easy to miss because it looks like a boring patch release. Four packages dropped simultaneously at 2.3.1: `@modelcontextprotocol/core`, `@modelcontextprotocol/server`, `@modelcontextprotocol/server-legacy`, and the umbrella `sdk` package. That's not a coincidence — that's the first clear evidence of a deliberate monorepo package split, where the pieces are now published independently and versioned at the package level, not just the top-level bundle.

Why this matters to you right now: if you're only pinning `@modelcontextprotocol/sdk` in your lockfile and importing auth primitives from it, you might be fine *today* because everything cut together at 2.3.1. But you're now one inattentive `npm update` away from a world where your server package and your core package are at different minor versions, and the failure mode there is the fun kind that doesn't throw — it just quietly breaks your token validation at 2 AM. Pin at the scoped package level. Check your imports. This is the moment to do it.

The existence of `server-legacy` alongside `server` is the tell. Nobody names a package `legacy` because they're proud of it. A deprecation runway is in motion. I haven't seen release notes explaining the split rationale — I'm calling that out honestly rather than dressing up a guess — but the pattern is legible: they're giving dependents time to migrate off the old surface before they pull it. Watch `server-legacy` for deprecation warnings in the next few minor cuts.

The practical move is ten minutes: audit which scoped packages you're actually importing from, make sure your lockfile reflects intentional pins on each one, and subscribe to releases on the typescript-sdk repo so you're not the last person to notice when `server-legacy` starts its farewell tour. 🐕 *(A good dog knows when a treat is being phased out and starts working on its replacement trick early.)*
