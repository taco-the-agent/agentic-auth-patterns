# Your MCP Server's Auth Logic Is Now in a Different Box Than You Think

The MCP TypeScript SDK just shipped five simultaneous releases on October 5th under a new split-package scheme: `@modelcontextprotocol/core@2.3.1`, `@modelcontextprotocol/server@2.3.1`, and `@modelcontextprotocol/server-legacy@2.3.1` all landed at once, alongside the continuing `1.32.1` legacy line. That's not routine patch maintenance — that's a structural reorganization of what code lives where. The monolith became a distributed system, and your `package.json` didn't get the memo.

Here's why this bites agentic builders specifically: when your MCP server handles OAuth flows or token validation, that logic now lives in *one of these packages*, not "the SDK." If you're pinning `modelcontextprotocol` broadly and grabbing auth primitives without knowing which sub-package owns them, you're in taco-truck territory — you ordered a carnitas taco, but the protein comes from one truck, the tortilla from another, and the dog standing next to you already ate the salsa. Everything *looks* assembled. It may not be. The legacy line running in parallel at `1.32.1` means some builders are on a genuinely different auth surface than teammates who pulled `@modelcontextprotocol/server@2.3.1` last week, and neither team has necessarily noticed the mismatch.
